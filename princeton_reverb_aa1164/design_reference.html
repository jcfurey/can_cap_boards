01

What this board does

Four independent B+ nodes, each filtered by its own radial electrolytic. The dirty power-supply returns (reservoir + screens) sit on their own ground, separate from the clean preamp/reverb returns, joined at a single star tie — that keeps the rectifier's charging-pulse currents out of the preamp's ground reference for lower hum, while still presenting one ground to the chassis. The reservoir is bumped to 47µF to firm up the low end (within the GZ34's ~60µF input limit); the preamp/reverb node is set to 33µF for a touch of headroom on an already-quiet amp, on a footprint that will still accept 47µF later. Screens and the PI/reverb-driver node stay at the stock 22µF, where extra capacitance buys nothing audible.

04

Board & mechanical layout

This mounts inside the chassis among the existing circuitry, not over the knockout. The wire-entry holes sit in a row along one edge — the edge facing your fiber board — so the existing B+ wires land straight across with no wrapping. Caps stand off the opposite face; mount on standoffs with both holes isolated. The empty can hole is best capped separately with a simple blanking plate or plug.

05

Layers & routing

Two copper layers, split by job. The side facing your fiber board carries every B+ trace and the wire-entry pads, so the existing wires land and solder on the face they come from — no wrapping around the board. The opposite side, under the caps and facing away from the fiber board, carries the two ground pours — power and preamp/reverb — split for low hum and joined only at the star.

B+ traces

Fat and short on the fiber-board side — ~2.5mm (100 mil) wide. The current is tiny; the width is for HV robustness and handling, not ampacity. Teardrop the pad junctions and round corners to resist etch defects and cracking.

Ground pours

Two pours on the cap side — GND-PWR under caps A·B, GND-PRE under caps C·D — each pulled back ≥2.5mm from B+. They meet only at the star link (one chassis tie), which keeps the rectifier's charging-pulse current off the preamp reference.

Clearances

Same-layer surface: ≥2.5mm B+↔B+ and B+↔ground (3mm where room allows). Opposite-layer through 1.6mm FR-4 is fine at 420V — for extra margin, clear the plane in the shadow directly beneath the B+ traces.

Holes & mounting

Wire-entry ~1.6mm hole / ~3.3mm pad — a generous ring for hand soldering. Stitch the plane to the GND pad with a few vias. Isolate both mount holes from copper (or make exactly one the ground tie) so two chassis screws can't form a ground loop.

06

Design rules to hold the line on

R1Creepage & clearance

Adjacent nodes can differ by 100–200V and the bus rides near 420V. Keep ≥2.5mm (0.1") bare between any two HV nodes and from HV to the ground pour / chassis metal — more if the board allows. A routed slot between nodes and/or conformal coat buys margin. IPC-2221 is the formal reference.

R2Grounding

Two pours: GND-PWR for the dirty returns (caps A·B + bleeder), GND-PRE for the clean preamp/reverb returns (caps C·D). They meet only at the star link — the single chassis tie. Fit the link for one ground wire, or omit it and run each pour to its own amp ground bus for a true split return. Either way, exactly one chassis connection; the rectifier center-tap lands on the power side at the star, never on the preamp pour.

R3Bleeder & service

The 200k bleeder drains node A when the amp is off. Downstream nodes drain through the amp's dropping resistors only while the board is connected. Always meter every section to zero before servicing; add a bleeder per node if you want each section to self-drain when the board is pulled.

R4Serviceability

Existing B+ wires solder into labeled wire-entry pads — leave a little slack so the board lifts clear when you desolder. Silkscreen node labels (A/B+1 … D/B+4), values, and polarity at every pad. Lay out C4 to accept up to 47µF so node D stays retunable with an iron. Want tool-free removal later? Add FASTON tabs in parallel with the wire pads.

R6Fab & copper

Well inside both JLCPCB and OSH Park 2-layer rules — our ≥2.5mm spacing dwarfs their ~0.15mm minimum, and 1.6mm wire-entry holes are routine. Standard 1oz copper at either fab clears your ½oz floor; ripple currents here are small, so 2oz buys little. OSH Park prices by area (~$5/in² for 3, US-made, ENIG), so keep it ~50mm; JLC is a flat tier up to 100×100mm for 5 pcs, so size is effectively free and you can spread out. Skip OSH Park's 2oz option — it's a thinner 0.8mm board, and you want 1.6mm rigidity for a cover.

R5Rectifier headroom

47µF reservoir is inside the GZ34's ~60µF input-cap limit — fine. If you ever convert to solid-state rectification, add inrush limiting (series resistance / soft-start), since 47µF hits the PT harder on a cold start.
